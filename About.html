About the Owner

Hey there! I'm Alex, the founder and head builder here at SkaterShop.

I've been skating local bowls and street rails for over 15 years. Frustrated with boards that snapped too easily or lacked proper pop, I decided to start crafting and curating gear that skaters can genuinely trust. Every skateboard in our store is tested by riders, for riders.

Whether you're dropping into a halfpipe for the first time or looking for a buttery-smooth cruiser, we've got your back.
